## 1. WHO I AM

This website, [ivankabandize.com](http://ivankabandize.com), is run by Ivan Kabandize, in Kampala, Uganda. I am the data controller for the personal data described here.

**About this policy.** This policy explains what personal data this website collects, why I collect it, who helps me handle it, how long I keep it, and the choices you have. I have written it in plain language on purpose. Where a legal word is unavoidable, I explain it.

**What "personal data" means.** It means any information that identifies you, or could be used to identify you, such as your name, your email address or an online identifier. In this policy "I" and "me" mean Ivan Kabandize, "you" means anyone who visits or uses the site, and "the site" means [ivankabandize.com](http://ivankabandize.com).

**What is required and what is optional.** I only ask for what I need. Where a field is marked optional you can leave it empty and nothing changes. If you leave out something that is required, I may not be able to do what you asked. For example, I cannot reply to your message without an email address. If you are not sure which details are required, ask me by writing to [privacy@ivankabandize.com](mailto:privacy@ivankabandize.com).

**Details about other people.** If you give me someone else's details, for example by mentioning a colleague in a message, please make sure they are happy for you to do so.

**This policy covers this site only.** It does not cover other websites, even if I link to them. You can print this page for your records using your browser's print command.

Questions or requests about your data: [privacy@ivankabandize.com](mailto:privacy@ivankabandize.com).

- **A legal duty.** Sometimes the law requires me to keep or share data, for example if a court orders it.

## 7. YOUR RIGHTS

You can ask me to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or stop how I use it, give it to you in a portable format, stop marketing to you, or withdraw consent at any time. Email [privacy@ivankabandize.com](mailto:privacy@ivankabandize.com). I may ask you to confirm who you are and aim to reply within 30 days, usually sooner. There is no fee unless a request is clearly excessive.

Here is what each right means in practice:

**Access.** You can ask whether I hold data about you and get a copy of it.

**Correction.** If something is wrong or out of date, you can ask me to fix it.

**Deletion.** You can ask me to delete your data. I will, unless I need to keep it for a legal reason, in which case I will tell you.

**Restriction.** You can ask me to pause how I use your data, for example while we check whether it is accurate.

**Objection.** Where I use your data because of my legitimate interest, you can object. For marketing you can object at any time without giving a reason.

**Portability.** You can ask for the data you gave me in a common, machine-readable format so you can take it elsewhere.

**Withdrawing consent.** Where I rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time through a written request..

**How to use these rights.** Email [privacy@ivankabandize.com](mailto:privacy@ivankabandize.com), preferably from the address you used on the site, and tell me what you would like. It is free. I will confirm who you are first, and aim to reply within 30 days. If I need longer, or cannot do what you ask, I will tell you why.

If you are unhappy, I would like the chance to put it right first. You may also complain to Uganda's Personal Data Protection Office (complaints portal: [pdpo.go.ug/file-complaint](http://pdpo.go.ug/file-complaint)), or, in the EU or UK, to your local data-protection authority.

## 8. SECURITY

Traffic to this site is encrypted. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Database access is restricted with row-level security and permissions, and administration is limited to me. No system is completely secure. If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, I will tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

**What this means in practice.**

**An encrypted connection.** Everything sent between your browser and the site travels over an encrypted connection (the padlock in your browser).

**Passwords.** I never see or store your actual password. It is turned into a scrambled code (a "salted hash") that cannot be turned back into the password.

**Who can see what.** The database has rules built in (called row-level security) so that each person can only reach the data they are allowed to see. Members-only articles are not sent in full to people who are not allowed to read them. Only I have administrator access.

## 11. WORDS USED IN THIS POLICY

**Personal data.** Any information that identifies you, or could be used to identify you.

**Usage data.** Information collected automatically when you visit, such as the pages you view, your browser and device type, and your country.

**Cookie.** A small file a website stores on your device. The [Cookie Policy](https://ivankabandize.com/cookie-policy) explains the ones this site uses.

**Data controller.** The person who decides why and how personal data is used. For this site, that is me.

**Data processor.** A company that handles data on my behalf and on my instructions, such as the host of the site or the service that sends emails.

**Consent.** Your free, clear choice to let me use your data for a stated purpose. You can withdraw it at any time.
